Welcome to The Front Range Edit.

Summer has a way of reminding us that time moves fast.

Between work, family, and everything we are building, it is easy to let good weekends disappear without much to show for them. I believe in working hard and making smart long-term moves, but I also believe the point of all that effort is to create a life you actually enjoy.

That is what this newsletter is about. A quick hello, a chance to stay connected, and a few worthwhile things happening around the Denver metro this month. Places to take your family, meet friends, get outside, support local businesses, and enjoy more of the community around you.

Hope you find something in here worth putting on the calendar.

3 Things Worth Having on Your Calendar

Denver, south metro, and Castle Rock, with a few things worth putting on the calendar this month.

 
Denver · Golden Triangle
Denver Chalk Art Festival
June 6–7, 2026 · 10 AM – 8 PM · 12th & Bannock · Free
More than 200 artists take over the Golden Triangle for a free weekend of live street art. Walk through the festival and watch chalk pieces come together in real time. Easy afternoon near Denver Art Museum, Clyfford Still Museum, Kirkland Museum, Evans School, and Leven Deli.
Why it's worth your time
It's free, colorful, walkable, and easy to do with family or friends. The Golden Triangle has plenty of reasons to stick around after.
South Metro · Greenwood Village
Greenwood Village Mobile Concert Series
Thursdays in June 2026 · 6 PM · Rotating GV Parks · Free
Live music in local parks every Thursday night in June, with free ice cream included. Each week features a different band at a different park, including Silo Park, Village Greens Park, Westlands Park, and Curtis Park. Bring a lawn chair or a blanket and make an easy evening of it.
Why it's worth your time
This is the kind of low-effort summer night people actually use. Bring a chair, grab a spot in the park, and let the kids run while the music plays.
Denver Markets
Farmers Markets
Saturdays & Sundays · Through October 2026 · Denver
Denver, University Hills: Saturdays, May 9–Oct 31 · 9 AM–1 PM · Wellshire Presbyterian Church, 2999 S. Colorado Blvd. Live music, local eats, drinks, and a relaxed neighborhood feel.
 
Denver, South Pearl Street: Sundays, May 3–Nov 8 · 9 AM–1 PM · 1500 block of South Pearl Street in Platt Park. Colorado-grown produce, local makers, prepared food, and one of Denver’s classic neighborhood market mornings.
Why it's worth your time
It supports local vendors and gives you a reason to get outside without overplanning the day. Good coffee, walk-around energy, and something for everyone.

June closed-sales data points to a Denver metro market that is steadier than the headlines can make it feel. The median closed price finished at $617,000, up 1.1% year over year, while 3,801 homes closed, down 5.4% from June 2025. Median days in MLS held at 19, which means well-priced homes are still moving, but buyers are not chasing quite as much volume as they were last year. As always, the bigger story depends on the neighborhood, property type, and price point.

Local Spotlight

Restaurant of the Month

 
 Platt Park · Denver
Margot
1551 S. Pearl St. · Platt Park Michelin Star · New American 5-course menu $95 · 12-course counter $165
Chef Justin Fulton's Platt Park restaurant earned a Michelin star just three months after opening in June 2025, one of the fastest coronations in Denver dining history. The tiny kitchen runs a 12-course tasting counter, a five-course tasting menu, and an à la carte dining room menu simultaneously. The focus is seasonal produce at its absolute peak. Standouts include the merguez tagliatelle with vegan pasta ribbons and lamb sausage Bolognese, and the Kinship toast with foie gras parfait that changes with the season.
What to order
Merguez tagliatelle · Kinship toast · Chicory "Caesar salad" · British cheddar with honey
